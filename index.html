About us

Our history, our roots

Ancestral Flavors and Fibers is a family business from the village of Túbuga, Mogotes, Santander. It was born from the love for our traditions, the honest work of the countryside, and the desire to keep our ancestral roots alive. Every product we make, from a small fique bag to an arepa with chicharrón, carries our history, our culture, and the pride of being peasants.
